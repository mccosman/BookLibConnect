Our Church

A Family in Faith, Rooted in Elloree

St Paul Baptist Church has long stood as a spiritual home for families along Cleveland Street in Elloree, South Carolina. For generations, our doors have opened to welcome the community in worship, prayer, celebration, and times of sorrow — a place where faith is lived out together, not alone.

We are an active Christian congregation grounded in Baptist tradition, proud to serve the local African American community and neighbors throughout Elloree. Whether you are visiting for the first time or you've called St Paul home your whole life, you are family here.

What We Believe

  • The Bible as the inspired, authoritative Word of God
  • Salvation through faith in Jesus Christ
  • The power of prayer and the presence of the Holy Spirit
  • The church as a family that worships, serves, and grows together

Sacred Ground

Our Church Cemetery & Memorial Services

Adjoining our sanctuary is the St Paul Baptist Church Cemetery, a resting place for generations of members and their families. It stands as a living part of our church grounds — a place of remembrance, quiet reflection, and homegoing services rooted in the same faith that guided our loved ones through life.

Our church family walks alongside those who are grieving, offering support for memorial and homegoing services, burial arrangements on church grounds, and pastoral care during times of loss.

Contact the Church Office for Arrangements

Memorial & Burial Support

  • Homegoing and memorial service coordination
  • Traditional burials on church cemetery grounds
  • Grief and pastoral care for families
  • Assistance honoring family plots and headstones

Our History

A Legacy Along Cleveland Street

St Paul Baptist Church sits along West Cleveland Street, a corridor woven into the history of Elloree's African American community — home also to neighboring Shiloh A.M.E. Church.

Generations of Faith

For decades, families in Elloree have been baptized, married, and celebrated within these walls, carrying forward a tradition passed down from parents and grandparents.

A Community Corridor

Alongside Shiloh A.M.E. Church, St Paul helps anchor Cleveland Street as a center of worship, gathering, and fellowship for the surrounding neighborhood.

Serving Together

Our church regularly joins with neighboring congregations for community gatherings, revivals, and pastoral functions that strengthen the whole of Elloree.
